# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report a vulnerability privately through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting for the repository that holds the affected code. Open the
repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. The report
is visible only to the repository owner.

If you cannot use private reporting, email contact@8bitswiser.com.

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Do not send credentials or
personal data; if you find a secret, send its location only, never its value.

## Scope

This policy applies to every repository in the 8bitswiser organization that does
not carry its own SECURITY.md.

It does not cover client systems, accounts or data outside these repositories,
or third-party services and dependencies. Report those to the upstream project.

## What to include

- What the flaw lets someone do, and where.
- The repository, path, and the version or commit you tested.
- Steps to reproduce, in as much detail as proves the point.
- Whether you want to be credited.

## What happens next

We confirm receipt, investigate, and tell you the outcome and the next step.
There is no paid bounty program.
